From rod to finished goods

Everything happens under one roof in Birgunj, so gauge and coating stay consistent batch to batch.

Wire drawing

Steel wire rod is pulled cold through a series of dies to reach precise, uniform diameters.

Galvanizing

Drawn wire is cleaned and zinc-coated (hot-dip or electro) for corrosion resistance.

Forming

Wire is cut, headed and shaped into nails, barbed strands, mesh and coils.

Packing & dispatch

Finished goods are bundled, weighed to standard and dispatched across Nepal.

Making wire on Nepal’s industrial front line

Nepal Metal & Wire Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a wire manufacturer based in Birgunj, the country’s main industrial hub and the busiest crossing point for trade with India. It’s the right place to source raw steel and reach markets across the Terai and the Kathmandu Valley.

We keep the whole line (drawing, galvanizing, nail-making and mesh) in one facility, so our customers get dependable quality and quick turnaround instead of long, uncertain lead times.
